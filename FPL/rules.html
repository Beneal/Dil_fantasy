Tournament rules

The full set of rules that applies to every Dil Fantasy gameweek tournament.

Entry

Entry and eligibility

  • The entry fee is 200 Birr for every gameweek tournament.
  • The entry fee is non-refundable once an entry has been submitted.
  • Participants must provide a valid FPL Manager ID.
  • Participants must be 18 or older.
  • One account per person. Fraudulent or duplicate accounts may be disqualified without refund.
  • Payment proof does not by itself confirm payment. Only entries verified by an administrator become confirmed participants.
Schedule

Timing

  • A new gameweek begins every Sunday at 12:00 AM EAT.
  • Registration opens Sunday at 12:00 AM EAT.
  • Registration closes Friday at 2:00 PM EAT.
  • Applications cannot be submitted after the deadline, under any circumstances.
  • All tournament times use Ethiopia Time (EAT, UTC+3), not the visitor's local time.
The deadline is enforced on the server as well as in the browser. Changing your device clock does not extend the window.
Scoring

How rankings are decided

  • Rankings are based on official Fantasy Premier League data. Dil Fantasy reads that data and does not modify it.
  • Gameweek standings use the points your FPL squad scores in that gameweek.
  • Ties are broken by total season points, then by overall FPL rank.
  • If the FPL service corrects a score after publication, standings are corrected to match.
Rewards

Rewards

  • Rewards follow the published tournament rules for that gameweek.
  • Rewards are paid to the reward account saved on your profile.
  • Payout status moves from pending to paid once the transfer is sent.
  • Reward account details are private and are never published.
Terms

Terms & conditions

Dil Fantasy is an independent Ethiopian competition platform for Fantasy Premier League managers. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to the Premier League or the official Fantasy Premier League game. All club and competition names remain the property of their owners.

Participation is a skill-based competition decided by Fantasy Premier League performance. Entries are accepted only through the process described on this site.

Dil Fantasy may update these rules. Changes apply from the gameweek following publication.

Privacy

Privacy

  • Public pages display manager names, FPL team names, ranks and points only.
  • Email addresses, phone numbers, ages, bank accounts, Telebirr accounts and payment records are never published.
  • Payment proof is used solely to verify an entry.
  • FPL access is read-only and limited to public performance data for your Manager ID.

Questions about your data? Contact support@dilfantasy.et.
