How this tournament is scored

Every entered manager is ranked by the points their official Fantasy Premier League squad scores in this gameweek. Dil Fantasy does not change, adjust or re-weight FPL scoring — it reads it.

Ties are broken by total season points, then by overall FPL rank.

Entering

Pay the 200 Birr entry fee through your own payment provider, then send the screenshot to the official Dil Fantasy Telegram group with your name and Manager ID. An administrator verifies it before your entry is confirmed.
