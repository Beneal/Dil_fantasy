The weekly rhythm

Sunday12:00 AM EAT
A new gameweek opens

The gameweek number increases by one, registration opens and the countdown resets. Nothing is manual.

Sun–FriOpen window
Registration is open

Apply, pay and send your proof any time during the window. Earlier is safer — verification takes time.

Friday2:00 PM EAT
Registration closes

Every apply and join button disables itself. Late applications cannot be accepted, for anyone.

Fri–SunMatch days
The tournament plays out

Your official FPL points decide the standings. The countdown switches to the next gameweek.

Payment

Paying the entry fee

Dil Fantasy has no online checkout. Nothing is charged on this site.

  1. Pay the 200 Birr entry fee through your own payment provider.
  2. Take a screenshot of the successful payment.
  3. Press Submit proof on Telegram in the app, which opens the official group.
  4. Send the screenshot with your full name and FPL Manager ID.
  5. Wait for an administrator to verify it.
  6. Your registration is confirmed once verification completes.
Submitting proof is not the same as approval. Only verified entries take part in the tournament, and the entry fee is non-refundable.
