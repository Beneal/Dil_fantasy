Tournaments

One tournament per gameweek. Registration opens every Sunday at 12:00 AM and closes every Friday at 2:00 PM, Ethiopia time. Entry is 200 Birr and non-refundable.

Buttons follow the clock. The moment Friday 2:00 PM EAT passes, every apply and join control on the site disables itself, and the next gameweek opens automatically on Sunday at 12:00 AM EAT.
